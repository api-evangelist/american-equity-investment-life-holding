# American Equity Investment Life Holding

> American Equity Investment Life Holding Company is a U.S. provider of guaranteed retirement
> income solutions, specializing in the design, development and sale of fixed indexed and
> fixed-rate annuities distributed through independent agents and broker-dealers. Its product

## API status

American Equity publishes **no public API, no OpenAPI or other machine-readable contract, no SDK,
no developer portal and no developer documentation**. Every integration surface is a
human-facing, authenticated web application for contracted financial professionals and for
contract owners. Do not attempt to construct API calls against these hosts.

- api.american-equity.com — a live AWS API Gateway that backs the MyPortal single-page app.
  Every path returns HTTP 403 `{"message":"Forbidden"}`; only `/ping` answers 200. It is a
  private backend, not a published product API. There is no anonymous contract to read.
- No /.well-known document is served on any American Equity host.
- No first-party package exists on npm, PyPI, RubyGems, NuGet, Maven Central, crates.io or
  pkg.go.dev under the American Equity or Eagle Life names (checked 2026-09-02).
